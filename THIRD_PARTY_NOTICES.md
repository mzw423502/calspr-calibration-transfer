- data/raw/materials/Au_Johnson.yml: refractiveindex.info database, public domain / CC0 1.0;
  Johnson and Christy (1972), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.6.4370.
- data/raw/materials/Olmon-ev.yml: same database and waiver; Olmon et al. (2012),
  DOI 10.1103/PhysRevB.86.235147, evaporated gold.

  DOI 10.1364/AO.37.005271, Lorentz-Drude model.

  LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt and the source attribution in NOTICE.
  scripts/calspr/figure_style_kernel.py contains the same imported plotting helper.
- Third-party runtime packages and .vendor binaries are not redistributed.
